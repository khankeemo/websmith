WEBsmith Social Icon Pack

SVG social-media icons for common platforms.

Facebook, Facebook Messenger, Instagram, WhatsApp, YouTube, LinkedIn, X, Twitter, TikTok, Telegram, Discord, Reddit, Pinterest, Snapchat, Twitch, GitHub, GitLab, Threads, Bluesky, Mastodon, Medium, Behance, Dribbble, Tumblr, Flickr, Vimeo, Spotify, SoundCloud, Skype, WeChat, Slack, Stack Overflow, Quora, VK, Patreon.
